MOH-100, the way it is filled now

This is the Kenya Ministry of Health community referral form as a web form: 22 fields, four blocks. It is the control condition for the pipeline in this repository. Fill it the way a community health volunteer would at the end of a shift, and compare the clock against a forty-five second voice note.

The timer starts when you touch the first field. Nothing is sent anywhere.
